About Me

A little more about where I come from, what brought me here, and what I enjoy outside of work and school.

My Journey

I grew up in Cincinnati, Ohio, and graduated from Taylor High School and Diamond Oaks Career Campus. Joining the United States Navy in 2017 gave me the chance to travel, meet people from across the country, and challenge myself in ways I never expected.

I spent four years on active duty aboard the USS Oak Hill and USS Vella Gulf, completing three deployments as a Gas Turbine Systems Technician. Life at sea taught me accountability, teamwork, and how to stay calm under pressure—lessons I still carry into work, school, and everyday life.

Coming Home

When my active-duty service ended in 2021, I stayed in Virginia for another two years working as a defense contractor. My first apartment was just a few minutes from Virginia Beach, and living near the ocean is something I still look back on fondly.

After nearly six years away, I moved back to Cincinnati to be closer to my family and pursue a new career. I enrolled at the University of Cincinnati to study Information Technology with a focus on cybersecurity.

I started that transition at Geek Squad, helping people solve technology problems while continuing my studies. Today, I work in IT support and security at Indivisible Partners as I continue toward my bachelor's degree.

Coming home also gave me a new appreciation for Cincinnati. I enjoy exploring hiking trails, trying local restaurants, spending time downtown, and discovering places I overlooked growing up.

Life Outside of Work

Outside of work and school, the gym is an important part of my routine. As a natural lifter, I enjoy working toward new goals, and training helps me look after both my physical and mental health.

I also enjoy playing guitar and singing, getting outside for hiking, running, pickleball, or snowboarding, and unwinding with a movie or a retro game.

I try to take at least one trip each year and enjoy experiencing other countries and cultures. Sometimes I travel solo, which gives me a chance to explore somewhere new at my own pace.

At home, I like building things and making improvements around the house. Painting gives me another way to slow down and be creative.
